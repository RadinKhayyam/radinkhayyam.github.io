# Personal homepage

A static, research-first homepage for Radin Khayyam. The approved brief and `content/site-copy.md` define its scope and copy.

Visitors can read research interests and publications, explore experience and projects, follow the personal timeline and sport record, download the supplied CV, and use the six contact destinations. The Blog starts empty.

Routes: Home, Research, Experience & Projects, Sport, Blog, and 404. No account, backend, tracking, form submission or deployment is required.
